Calculation semantics retained from the supplied model:
- Total Citations counts distinct original/citing publication pairs in the link table.
- Cited Publications counts distinct original publication IDs in that link table.
- Percent cited divides Cited Publications by Publications.
- Recent Citations uses citing publications from the latest citing year and prior year.
- Current Year series means the latest publication year in the selected data,
  which may differ from the calendar year.
- Business area uses Business Unit, following the existing overview's business-area visual.
- Disclosure type uses publication_type; open access route uses OA Group.
- No impact-factor field exists in the model; that position says Not available.
